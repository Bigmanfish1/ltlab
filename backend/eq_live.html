One-way implication
B is stronger than A. Every behaviour satisfying B also satisfies A, but not the reverse.

≢
This single behaviour proves A and B are not equivalent: reading it as one infinite run, it satisfies A but not B. Since equivalence means agreeing on every behaviour, one disagreement is enough. The difference is liveness-style: no single state breaks B (FGp). It fails only in the limit, because the loop repeats forever without ever fulfilling it.
